THE SHOOTING GAME FOR HISTORY - USER INSTRUCTIONS

WELCOME
This is a 3D castle exploration and battle game set in a medieval environment.
Choose your adventure: fight enemies or peacefully explore the castle.

GETTING STARTED
1. Open the game in your web browser
2. Select a game mode from the start menu:
   - Start Game (Fight Enemies) - Battle mode with 15 enemies to defeat
   - Explore Castle (Peaceful) - Free exploration without combat

CONTROLS

Desktop/Laptop:
Click anywhere to lock mouse cursor
W, A, S, D keys - Move forward, left, backward, right
Mouse - Look around and aim
SPACEBAR - Jump
E key - Shoot (battle mode only)

Mobile/Tablet:
Joystick (bottom left) - Move character
Swipe on screen - Look around and aim
Jump button (top right) - Jump
Shoot button (bottom right) - Shoot (battle mode only)

GAME MODES

Battle Mode:
Defeat all 15 enemies inside the castle
Each enemy takes 3 shots to kill
Enemies shoot back and deal 20 damage per hit
You have 100 health points
Kill counter shows your progress (0/15)
Win by defeating all enemies
Respawn at castle entrance if you die

Explore Mode:
Peacefully walk around the castle
No enemies or combat
Explore all 7 towers with spiral staircases
Walk across the drawbridge
Visit the houses and farms

MAP FEATURES
Main Central Tower - Tallest structure with spiral stairs to the top
6 Side Towers - Each has its own spiral staircase
Houses - 4 houses with doors you can enter
Farms - Barley fields near the houses
Moat - Water surrounding the castle (causes death if you fall in)
Drawbridge - Opens automatically when you approach
Gate - Opens when you get close to the entrance

IMPORTANT WARNINGS
MOAT = DEATH - Stay on the bridge when crossing the moat
If you fall in the moat, you will drown after 3 seconds
Enemies respawn when you die in battle mode
Health displays in top left corner

TIPS
Use the spiral stairs to climb towers and get better vantage points
In battle mode, keep moving to avoid enemy fire
The gate opens automatically when you get close
You can climb small steps automatically while walking
Watch your health bar - it turns orange at 60HP, red at 30HP
In explore mode, take time to visit all the towers and houses

TROUBLESHOOTING
If controls don't work, click on the game window to activate pointer lock
If you get stuck, try jumping
If stuck in moat, wait to respawn at entrance
If enemies don't spawn, refresh and select Battle Mode again

Enjoy your adventure!
